##  Topic Overview: Data & Security
Data is one of the most valuable assets in the digital age and protecting it is no longer optional. This repository provides a structured exploration of Data & Security, covering everything from core definitions to real-world threats and modern defense mechanisms.
We examine the difference between Data and Information, the principles of the CIA Triad and Zero Trust, and the most dangerous cyber threats of today — Phishing, Malware, and Ransomware. From there, we move into the technologies used to fight back, including Encryption, Authentication, Authorization, and Identity & Access Management (IAM).
The exploration also highlights the growing concern around the rapid rise of data usage against the lack of proper security practices, supported by real datasets, visual charts, and structured analysis.
Every concept in this exploration reflects a real challenge faced by individuals, organizations, and governments in today's connected world.

### Key Areas of Focus:
**Data & Security** — A foundational look at what data and security mean in today's digital landscape.

**Definition (Data and Security)** — Clear, precise definitions to build understanding before exploring deeper concepts.

**Data vs Information** — The critical difference between raw data and meaningful, actionable information.

**CIA Triad & Zero Trust** — Core security principles: Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability, and the Zero Trust model.

**Threats & Attacks** — Real-world dangers including Phishing, Malware, and Ransomware and how they operate.

**Security Technologies** — Modern tools and defenses used to protect systems and digital infrastructure.

**Types of Data Security** — Physical, administrative, and technical approaches to securing data.

**Encryption** — How encryption transforms sensitive data into unreadable formats for protection.

**Authentication & Authorization** — Verifying identity and controlling what users are permitted to access.

**Identity & Access Management (IAM)** — Managing digital identities and enforcing access control at scale.

**Data Classification & Sensitivity** — Categorizing data by sensitivity level to apply appropriate protection.

**increase of Data Usage & Lack of Security** — The widening divide between growing data usage and insufficient security practices.

**Importance, Benefits & Best Practices** — Why data security matters and how to implement it effectively.

**Data Table & Chart** — Real-world security statistics presented through structured tables and visual charts.
